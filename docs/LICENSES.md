# Licensing decisions

The existing software [MIT License](../LICENSE) remains unchanged. Lattice
CourseLab separates educational content from software so source obligations
are visible when course packages grow.

## Original content

[LICENSE-CONTENT](../LICENSE-CONTENT) selects CC BY 4.0 for new original course
content. This permits broad reuse while requiring attribution and marking
changes. The legal terms are the [official CC BY 4.0 legal code](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/legalcode),
not this summary. Original software and implementation documentation continue
to use MIT.

The prototype placed original course text in the MIT-licensed repository.
Those historical permissions are retained for preserved text, including when
moved into a course package. Such text is offered under both MIT and CC BY 4.0;
recipients can choose either. New contributions are CC BY 4.0 unless explicitly
identified differently. This is an additional grant, not a revocation or a
retroactive restriction.

## External content

The initial source registry is for links and factual curriculum alignment.
It contains no imported university material and does not authorize ingestion
of linked pages. Johns Hopkins catalogue/AAP pages reserve copyright, so
their course descriptions and assets are not copied. Their public topic and
prerequisite facts inform an original knowledge map with no affiliation or
equivalency claim.

Applicable OCW material may permit adaptation under its current CC BY-NC-SA
4.0 terms. Any future approved adaptation stays under that license and includes
complete attribution, a license link, and an account of modifications. It
cannot be relabeled as CourseLab CC BY 4.0 content. All-rights-reserved or
excluded assets are not imported. Asset-level review also identifies special
third-party notices that must be preserved.

Track exceptions and assets in [content/SOURCES_AND_LICENSES.md](../content/SOURCES_AND_LICENSES.md)
and [the source registry](../content/sources/registry.json). A license label is
not a substitute for identifying the author, source, exact asset, and changes.

## Contributor obligations

Contributors must identify which portions they created and which, if any,
come from external sources. Accepting a contribution must not silently change
a license boundary. Preserve existing copyright and attribution notices.
Record new asset rights before merging the asset, and keep notices available
in the rendered course, downloadable package, and source files. Credentials,
user data, institutional trademarks, and learner submissions are not covered
by these repository grants.

No institutional endorsement, accreditation, credit, review approval, or
learning outcome follows from any source link or content license.
