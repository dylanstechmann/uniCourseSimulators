## Selected circuit and numerical checks

- A simultaneous nodal solution checks unloaded and loaded port voltages. A suppressed-source test checks resistance, branch powers match delivered source power, and maximum-power matching is contrasted with small voltage-loading error.
- A bisection solution of the finite feedback relation checks gain and input difference. Bias-current polarity and a demanded output beyond its stipulated rail expose invalid linear operating assumptions.
- An independently evaluated four-resistor difference network checks common-mode leakage under mismatched ratios. A small predicted output with invalid terminal input range is a separate counterexample.
- Component RK4 integration of a passive two-section ladder checks stored capacitor energy, source work and resistor heat at three times. Phasor node balances distinguish its loaded transfer from an ideal buffered cascade.
- A transformed numerical integral checks equivalent white-noise bandwidth. Constructed orthogonal components check covariance effects on sums and differences at four correlations. Finite differences check calibration sensitivities and a correlated-parameter counterexample.
- All ideal quantizer bin centers and a ramp check code/error bounds. Repeated DC error is a counterexample to assuming white independent quantization noise. Component integration checks acquisition settling; discrete samples check alias equivalence and exact Nyquist phase ambiguity.
- All 32 CSV cells are recalculated directly from the committed reports. Independent OLS fits compare interior calibration with a compromised all-point fit, and normalized baseline-excess fits allow the disclosed six-decimal data rounding.

The preserved 150 Hz/300 Hz Nyquist item is a nominal two-times threshold, not arbitrary-phase recovery at the exact boundary or a practical anti-alias/acquisition guarantee. This limitation is documented without changing the preserved question/key. All other preserved numeric results use their existing tolerances and prompt-rounded inputs.

## Lab construction and case scope

There are 48 constructed outputs in sixteen groups. Settled differential references −15,−5,0,+5,+15 mV are supplied independently for gain 20 and gain 100 conditions. The settled offset is 0.10 V and output clamp is ±1 V. Deterministic perturbations −0.001,zero,+0.001 V are added before clipping and rounding to six decimals. Thus both high-gain endpoint groups have three identical rail outputs; these do not establish equal original amplitudes or actual precision. The unsaturated interior groups recover gains 20/100 and offset 0.10 V. Fitting all five high-gain means as linear instead yields compromised gain 70/intercept 0.06 V.

Common-mode references −1/+1 V at differential zero yield coefficient 0.010 V/V and nominal CMRR 80 dB under an independently stipulated valid input range. A separately stated zero-input baseline b(t)=0.10+0.30exp(−t/0.50) supplies a conditional 0.50 s decay; settled and drifting states must not be pooled. The preserved movement-clipping/rest-drift case remains self-assessment. Synthetic controls constrain selected electrical explanations without unique real electrode/biological identification or a subject procedure.
