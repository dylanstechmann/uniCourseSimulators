## Lab construction and scope

The planar arm has link lengths 0.3 and 0.2 m, shoulder angle zero and elbow angles 90, 30, 10 and 2 degrees. Desired velocities are fixed-base (−0.01,0) and (0,0.01) m/s. Names inward and tangent describe those base directions, not exact radial/tangential directions at every bent posture. Each posture has a frozen Jacobian, with no integration between postures. Unconstrained joint rates solve J qdot=v. The common scale is min(1,0.5/max|qdot|), keeping both commanded joint speeds within 0.5 rad/s and preserving the requested Cartesian direction under the linear model.

For the 2-degree inward request, unconstrained rates are approximately −0.954542 and 2.387227 rad/s; scale 0.209448 gives elbow command 0.5 rad/s and inward speed 2.094480 mm/s. Independently clipping both joints gives a different Cartesian direction, (0,−0.15) m/s. All tangent requests have rates (1/30,−1/30) rad/s and scale one. Three deterministic report variants per group add x offsets −0.02,0,+0.02 mm/s and y offsets +0.01,0,−0.01 mm/s. These are synthetic reporting variations, not calibrated sensor noise or evidence of a fault. Commands are rounded to nine decimals, reports to six; upload checks use group counts and component means.

No dynamics, current limit, friction, backlash or actual sensing is included in the lab. Static force mapping, speed-limited inverse motion and actuator/dynamic requirements must remain separate.
