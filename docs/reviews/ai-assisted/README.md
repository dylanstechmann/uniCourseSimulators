found and what was fixed. These checks recompute answers and look for inconsistencies, overclaims and
stale text. They are not independent: AI systems wrote much of the content. They are not human review
and change no maturity or review label. See the [reviewer guide](../../REVIEWER_GUIDE.md) for the kinds of
review that do.
