Three H6/H7 numeric keys were repaired: two CSV protein means had been rounded more coarsely than their declared tolerance, and one rescue contrast had been computed from nominal percentages instead of supplied integer counts. This is a foundation for evidence tracking, not the complete pipeline binding real independent numerical/test artifacts and human review. The keys, answer-specific tests, logs and source/test hashes remain outside Git under `courselab-session-artifacts/codex-biology-completion-2026-10-09` and the H5–H8 artifact roots.
