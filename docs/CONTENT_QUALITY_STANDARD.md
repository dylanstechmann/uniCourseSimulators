# Content quality standard

Maturity applies to a specific content version.

| Status | Evidence |
| --- | --- |
| catalog-only | Accurate metadata, dependencies, sources, limitations |
| outlined | Structured syllabus/plan with undeveloped areas stated |
| partial | Usable original material with explicit coverage gaps |
| beta | Coherent developed course undergoing validation/review |
| complete | Every full-course gate and actual human review passes |
| externally reviewed | Complete gates plus named qualified independent review of version |

No current package is complete. Preserved-seed brevity exceptions never qualify for complete.

## Full-course gates

1. Schema, stable references, acyclic dependencies, history/accessibility metadata.
2. Twelve to fifteen weeks or reviewed equivalent with credible workload.
3. Substantial original explanation, assumptions, examples, experimental/data reasoning and omissions.
4. Objective coverage through practice, homework, laboratory/simulation/data/design activities, midterm, cumulative final and authentic project.
5. Variant specifications/rubrics, answer completeness, seed reproducibility, independent recalculation, units/tolerances and grader mutation checks.
6. Source/license/asset provenance, checked links and no excluded reuse.
7. Placeholder/trivial/duplicate detection plus human near-duplicate review.
8. Accessible content and keyboard/focus behavior with automated checks.
9. Frontend/backend/E2E tests, solution authorization, persistence and secret checks.
10. Actual human review of version, with unresolved deficiencies blocking completion.

Missing evidence is failure. Counts cannot replace depth. Review scientific correctness, causal reasoning, controls, uncertainty, rigor and grading validity. Never fabricate review, outcomes, accreditation or equivalence.

The 14-week Cell and Molecular Biology slice requires substantive weekly lessons, eight homework sets, three data/lab/simulation activities, midterm, final, project, variants, cards, misconceptions and objective-linked feedback/provenance. Its four seeds remain partial until this is developed and reviewed.
