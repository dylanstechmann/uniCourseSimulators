# Curriculum map

The 25 partial packages cover mathematics/statistics, physics, chemistry, biology, computing, engineering and specialized biomedicine. Several merge subjects that need separate full packages. Four suggested paths are retained for compatibility.

Milestone 4 adds catalog-only/outlined entries and chains for Calculus I–III, linear algebra, differential equations, probability/statistics, numerical methods, mechanics, electricity/magnetism, statics, dynamics, mechanics of materials, thermodynamics, fluids, heat/mass transfer, materials, circuits I–II, analog/digital electronics, signals, control, instrumentation, embedded systems, mechanical design, robotics/mechatronics and engineering design/experimental methods.

Required pathways: science/mathematics foundations; electrical/computer engineering; mechanical engineering/robotics; biomedical engineering; tissue engineering/regenerative medicine; geroscience/aging; drug discovery/translation; and Johns Hopkins regenerative/stem-cell prerequisite knowledge.

The JHU map includes organic chemistry, biochemistry, molecular biology and cell biology; advanced developmental biology, gene therapy, regenerative medicine, bioethics, stem cells, tissue-engineered drug discovery, biotherapeutic manufacturing and laboratory concepts. Official references/caveats: [CURRICULUM_REFERENCE_NOTES.md](CURRICULUM_REFERENCE_NOTES.md). These independent maps imply no affiliation, admission eligibility, prerequisite equivalence or transfer credit.

Every new entry states its maturity. Breadth is not an excuse to invent shallow courses. Original instruction/assessments require separate development and review.
