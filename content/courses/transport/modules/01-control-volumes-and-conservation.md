# Control volumes and conservation

Conservation balances are the backbone of transport. For a species in a control volume, accumulation equals inflow minus outflow plus generation minus consumption. Steady state means accumulation is zero, not that fluxes vanish. Energy balances include heat, work, enthalpy flow, and kinetic or potential terms when relevant. The control volume and assumptions should be drawn before algebra; neglected terms should be justified by scale or operating regime.
