# Diffusion, reaction, and characteristic length

Fick's first law is J=−D∇C; flux points down the concentration gradient. A characteristic diffusion time is td≈L²/D, while a first-order reaction time is tr≈1/k. Their ratio defines a Damköhler-type comparison that indicates whether diffusion or reaction limits a process. Boundary conditions and geometry determine full solutions. In a thick construct, oxygen consumption can create a core deficit even if surface concentration is adequate.
