# Current, resistance, and RC transients

Current is charge flow per time. Kirchhoff's current law expresses charge conservation at a node; voltage law follows energy conservation around a loop. Ohm's law V=IR applies to an ohmic element within its operating regime. For a series RC circuit, τ=RC and a step response approaches its final value exponentially. Instrument input impedance can load a high-impedance sensor and alter the quantity being measured.
