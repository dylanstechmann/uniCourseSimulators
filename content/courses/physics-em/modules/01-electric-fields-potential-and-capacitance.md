# Electric fields, potential, and capacitance

Electric field E is force per unit positive test charge; potential difference is energy per charge. For a parallel-plate capacitor, C≈εA/d when edge effects are small. Stored energy is U=½CV². Conductors redistribute charge until electrostatic equilibrium is reached. In physiological systems, membranes act as thin capacitors separating ionic solutions; fields and potentials depend on both charge distribution and boundary conditions.
