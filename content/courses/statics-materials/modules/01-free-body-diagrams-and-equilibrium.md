# Free-body diagrams and equilibrium

A static body satisfies ΣF=0 and ΣM=0. Choose a system boundary, draw applied loads and support reactions, and use moments to remove unknown forces when possible. Distributed loads can be replaced by resultants acting at their centroids. A support model is an assumption: a pin resists translation but not moment; a fixed support resists both. Check that the number of independent equilibrium equations is adequate for the unknown reactions.
