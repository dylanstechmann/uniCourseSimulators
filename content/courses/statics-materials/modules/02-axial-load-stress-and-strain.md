# Axial load, stress, and strain

For an axial member under force F, average normal stress is σ=F/A. In linear elasticity, strain ε=σ/E and extension ΔL=FL/(AE). These relations assume a suitable material model, small deformation, uniform section, and boundary conditions that justify average stress. Stress concentrations around holes or interfaces require local analysis. In soft tissue and hydrogels, E can depend on strain, rate, hydration, and loading direction.
