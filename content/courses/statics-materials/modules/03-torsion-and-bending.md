# Torsion and bending

For a circular shaft in elastic torsion, τ=Tr/J, where T is applied torque, r the radial coordinate, and J the polar second moment. For beam bending, σ=My/I, with maximum magnitude at the outer fiber. Deflection and stress are different design constraints: a component may remain below yield yet be too flexible. Composite, porous, or anisotropic biomaterials require appropriate section properties and material axes.
