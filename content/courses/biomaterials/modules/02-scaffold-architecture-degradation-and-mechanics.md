# Scaffold architecture, degradation, and mechanics

Scaffold design couples pore size, interconnectivity, surface area, degradation, and mechanical support. Higher porosity can improve transport and cell access but reduce stiffness and strength. Degradation products can change local pH or become toxic; degradation should be synchronized with tissue formation and load transfer. Characterize wet mechanics over time, not only dry initial values. A scaffold is a dynamic participant in remodeling, not an inert container.
