# Cells, signals, and tissue-specific strategies

Tissue engineering typically combines cells, scaffolds, signaling cues, and a culture or implantation environment. Cell source affects immune compatibility, expansion, potency, and donor variability. Growth factors and genes can be spatially or temporally delivered; sustained exposure is not always beneficial. Mechanical loading can promote maturation or damage depending on tissue and stage. A design rationale should connect each component to a measurable functional criterion rather than equating marker expression with tissue function.
