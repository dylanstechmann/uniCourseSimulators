# Material properties at the biological interface

Biological fluids condition a material surface rapidly through adsorption of proteins, lipids, and ions. Cells therefore interact with a dynamic interfacial layer rather than a pristine polymer alone. Surface energy, charge, roughness, chemistry, and mechanics influence adsorption and adhesion, but their effects depend on the biological milieu. Characterize bulk and surface properties separately and test at relevant protein composition, flow, sterilization state, and timescale.
