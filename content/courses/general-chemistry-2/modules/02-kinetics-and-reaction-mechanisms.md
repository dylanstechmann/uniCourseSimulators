# Kinetics and reaction mechanisms

A rate law is determined experimentally and is not generally inferred from the balanced overall equation. For an elementary step, exponents reflect molecularity, but mechanisms can contain intermediates and rate-limiting steps. The integrated first-order law is ln([A]t/[A]0) = −kt, with half-life ln 2/k. Arrhenius behavior k = A exp(−Ea/RT) predicts faster rates at higher temperature for positive activation energy, while catalysts provide an alternate pathway with lower Ea.
