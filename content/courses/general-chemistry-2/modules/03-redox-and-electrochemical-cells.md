# Redox and electrochemical cells

Oxidation loses electrons and reduction gains them. Standard reduction potentials are intensive properties and are not multiplied by stoichiometric coefficients. For a galvanic cell under standard conditions, E°cell = E°cathode − E°anode and ΔG° = −nFE°cell. Under nonstandard conditions, the Nernst equation adjusts potential for activities. Biological redox couples depend on pH, concentrations, compartmentation, and coupling to other reactions.
