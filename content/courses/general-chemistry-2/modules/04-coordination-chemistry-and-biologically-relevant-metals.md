# Coordination chemistry and biologically relevant metals

Transition-metal ions bind ligands through donor atoms, forming coordination complexes. Coordination number and ligand-field effects influence geometry, color, magnetic properties, and redox potential. In proteins, the local environment can tune reactivity far beyond what the free metal ion exhibits. Metal speciation also depends on pH and competing ligands; total elemental concentration is not the same as bioavailable or catalytically active concentration.
