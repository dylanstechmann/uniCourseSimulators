# Acid-base equilibria and buffers

For HA ⇌ H⁺ + A⁻, Ka = [H⁺][A⁻]/[HA]. In a buffer with appreciable amounts of both forms, pH = pKa + log₁₀([A⁻]/[HA]) approximates the equilibrium relation. The approximation is most useful when concentrations are well above water autoionization and activity effects are modest. Strong acid or base first reacts stoichiometrically with buffer components; update moles before applying the ratio. Buffer capacity depends on total concentration and is largest near pH = pKa.
