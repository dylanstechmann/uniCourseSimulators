# Protein structure and molecular recognition

Primary sequence constrains local structure and folding but does not uniquely specify a single rigid state in every environment. Secondary and tertiary structure emerge from backbone geometry, hydrophobic packing, hydrogen bonding, electrostatics, and solvent. Ligand binding is governed by ΔG and can involve conformational selection or induced fit. The dissociation constant Kd reports equilibrium occupancy in a simple 1:1 model; a low Kd indicates tight binding but not necessarily fast association or catalytic efficiency.
