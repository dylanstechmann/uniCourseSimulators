# Enzyme mechanisms and inhibition

Enzymes stabilize transition states and use acid-base, covalent, metal-ion, or proximity/orientation strategies. In the simplest Michaelis-Menten model, competitive inhibition increases apparent Km without changing Vmax; pure noncompetitive inhibition lowers Vmax with unchanged Km. Real inhibitors may be mixed, tight-binding, time-dependent, or allosteric, so model fit and assay conditions matter. kcat/Km is a useful low-substrate measure of catalytic efficiency, bounded by diffusion for many enzymes.
