# Bioenergetics and central carbon metabolism

Glycolysis converts glucose to pyruvate while producing ATP and NADH. Under aerobic conditions, pyruvate oxidation and the citric-acid cycle generate reduced cofactors; electron transport transfers electrons to oxygen and pumps protons. ATP synthase couples proton return to phosphorylation. The overall ATP yield per glucose depends on shuttle systems, leak, cell type, and accounting convention. Carbon and electron balances provide a more reliable framework than memorizing one universal ATP number.
