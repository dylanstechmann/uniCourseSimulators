# Pathway regulation and metabolic integration

Metabolic flux is distributed across pathways and is not controlled by one universal rate-limiting enzyme. Regulation occurs through substrate availability, allosteric effectors, phosphorylation, enzyme abundance, compartmentation, and hormonal signals. Mass balance constrains steady-state intermediates, while isotope tracing can reveal carbon flow through parallel pathways. A metabolite pool size alone does not reveal flux: it can rise because production increased or consumption fell.
