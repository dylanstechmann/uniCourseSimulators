# Calculus I: Differential and Integral Calculus

**Maturity: partial. Version: 0.4.0.** This personal hobby and learning project is developed with substantial AI assistance. It is not an accredited university course.

Limits, derivatives and integrals describe local change and accumulation, with constructed examples in growth, transport, geometry and measurement.

## Prerequisites

Algebra, functions and trigonometry. Structured prerequisite relationships appear in the manifest and remain subject to author review. This package establishes no university prerequisite equivalency.

## Authored outcomes

- Evaluate limits and justify continuity claims.
- Differentiate composite and implicit functions.
- Use derivatives to analyze and optimize models.
- Interpret definite integrals as accumulated change.

## Assessment and study policy

There are 14 readings, 92 public practice items and 48 cards, plus the original self-assessment case. Numeric, single-choice, CSV-upload, structured-choice and multiple-select checks support the new material. One preserved symbolic item supports bounded rational expressions only; it is not general symbolic calculus grading. Practice points are feedback, with unlimited retries and no institutional grade, university credit or mastery certification. Written discussion criteria are not automatically graded prose. Assessment outcome lists reflect the objectives their items assess; the prototype links remain provisional.

## Current limitations

Four compact prototype readings remain. There is no graded homework sequence, midterm, final or graded integrative project. One synthetic lab is not a laboratory sequence. Workload, accessibility and content have not been reviewed by a qualified person. Numerical bounds require the stated smoothness, domains and noise assumptions. All washout signals are constructed; the course gives no real clearance estimates, experimental protocols or device specifications. The package remains partial and formative-only.
