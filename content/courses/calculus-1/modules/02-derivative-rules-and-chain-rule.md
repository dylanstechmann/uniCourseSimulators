# Derivative rules and chain rule

The derivative is a local slope and instantaneous rate. Product and quotient rules handle composed arithmetic; the chain rule handles nested dependence: d/dx f(g(x)) = f′(g(x))g′(x). In a physical model, derivatives inherit units: if position is meters and time seconds, velocity has units m/s. The chain rule is especially important in biological models where a measured output depends on a hidden state that itself depends on time or dose.
