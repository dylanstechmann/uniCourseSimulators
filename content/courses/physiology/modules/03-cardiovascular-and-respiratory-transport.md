# Cardiovascular and respiratory transport

Bulk flow moves blood or air through conduits; diffusion moves solutes down concentration gradients across exchange surfaces. For laminar flow in a cylindrical tube, Poiseuille resistance scales as R = 8μL/(πr⁴), so small radius changes strongly affect flow at fixed pressure drop. Gas exchange depends on surface area, barrier thickness, solubility, and perfusion. Ventilation-perfusion mismatch can limit oxygen transfer even when total ventilation is adequate.
