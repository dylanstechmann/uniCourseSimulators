# Renal, endocrine, and integrated regulation

Renal function combines filtration, reabsorption, secretion, and excretion. Clearance Cx = Ux·V̇/Px expresses the plasma volume cleared of a substance per unit time, subject to steady-state and sampling assumptions. Endocrine signals act through receptor occupancy and downstream transduction; hormone concentration alone does not equal tissue response. Integrating organ systems requires tracing the manipulated variable, compensatory response, and any cost of compensation.
