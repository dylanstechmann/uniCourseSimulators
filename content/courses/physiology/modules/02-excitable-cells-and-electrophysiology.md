# Excitable cells and electrophysiology

The membrane behaves approximately as a capacitor in parallel with ion-selective conductances. Ionic current follows Iᵢ = gᵢ(Vm − Eᵢ), with sign determined by the chosen current convention. Opening a conductance drives voltage toward that ion's reversal potential. Action potentials arise from voltage- and time-dependent conductance changes; refractory behavior and propagation depend on channel kinetics and cable properties, not simply on a fixed voltage threshold.
