# Cell and Molecular Biology: proposed scope and assessment crosswalk

**Crosswalk status:** planning document, course version 0.17.0. It is not a set of completed assignments. **The course remains partial.** Weeks 1–7 have two original topic lessons each and public formative items; week 5 also has the first virtual data-analysis lab, and weeks 4 and 7 retain compact prototype readings. Week 8 has an ungraded cumulative practice set, not a midterm. Weeks 9–14 have original lesson pairs and formative practice sets.

## Intended course outcomes

- **O1 — Structure and function:** Relate molecular structure and chemical environment to plausible biological function, and state what those observations do not establish.
- **O2 — Quantitative models:** Apply quantitative physical and biochemical models while checking assumptions, units, and limits.
- **O3 — Information flow:** Trace genetic information through DNA replication, RNA processing, regulation, translation, and phenotype.
- **O4 — Experimental reasoning:** Evaluate perturbations, measurements, controls, uncertainty, alternatives, and the scope of causal claims.

The IDs below refer to the course outcomes in `course.json`. New lesson-objective IDs are created only as corresponding instruction and assessments are authored; this outline does not substitute for that mapping.

**Sequencing decision:** preserve weeks 1–7 as the teaching block on molecular foundations, replication/repair, and chromatin/transcription. Week 8 offers a cumulative 12-question formative practice set assembled from public answer-bearing items already in the bank. It is delivered through an answer-free, enrollment-version-pinned learner API and gives per-question feedback. Weeks 9 and 10 add original reading pairs and formative sets with eight and seven questions respectively; these remain practice and supply no evidence of summative readiness. No exam questions, protected exam answer key or exam rubric, release policy, assessment record, dates, or deadlines have been authored. Week 8 is not a delivered exam.

## Scope comparator and provenance

The sequence is an original planning proposal informed at the topic level by public MIT OpenCourseWare comparators. These are **link-only comparators** in `../../sources/registry.json`; the course does not reproduce or adapt their lectures, figures, problems, exams, or text. Scientific and methods sources are linked in the source registry; the week-7 examples are original synthetic data and do not reproduce any cited table or figure. No MIT endorsement, course equivalence, prerequisite equivalence, or credit transfer is implied. Every new module has a per-reading provenance entry in `source-map.json`; third-party assets require separate affirmative license review.
