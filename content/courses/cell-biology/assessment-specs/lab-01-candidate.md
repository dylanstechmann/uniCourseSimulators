### cell-biology-lab-01-candidate:case-01 — 12 proposed points

Virtual lab 1: original bounded data evidence. Authored form A. Four independently prepared cultures have input marker activities [100, 120, 140, 160] and collected fraction activities [50, 60, 70, 80] in matched arbitrary activity units, indexed in the same culture order. Each culture has two technical image fields. Channel overlap occurs within a conventional optical spot and single-label controls show cross-talk. A contaminating compartment marker is present; missing fractions and integrity measurements are absent. Calculate per-culture recovery then its equal-culture mean.

Virtual lab 1: original bounded data evidence. Authored form B. Four independently prepared cultures have input marker activities [100, 120, 140, 160] and collected fraction activities [60, 70, 80, 90] in matched arbitrary activity units, indexed in the same culture order. Each culture has two technical image fields. Channel overlap occurs within a conventional optical spot and single-label controls show cross-talk. A contaminating compartment marker is present; missing fractions and integrity measurements are absent. Calculate per-culture recovery then its equal-culture mean.

**criterion-1 (4 points):** Which image conclusion/control is justified?

1. Overlap proves binding, so omit controls.
2. Correct cross-talk with single-label controls; optical overlap alone does not prove direct molecular binding.
3. Count eight fields as eight independently prepared cultures.

**criterion-2 (4 points):** Calculate equal-culture mean marker recovery as percent. Report exactly 3 significant figures.

Response scale: `%`; absolute tolerance 0.005, relative tolerance 0.



**criterion-3 (4 points):** Which next fractionation design addresses missing evidence?

1. Use only the enriched marker to declare purity.
2. Measure matched input, all fractions, multi-marker contamination and integrity controls; recovery is distinct from purity.
3. Replace input with exposure time and ignore missing fractions.
