## Question and units

Investigate how the named signal and matrix conditions are associated with RNA abundance, recovered protein, signaling trajectory, marker-positive fraction and a separately defined functional-event proxy. Ask a question the available measurements can answer, distinguish a layer from a mechanism, and propose a matched follow-up. The four conditions control, signal_high, matrix_high and both form the main descriptive factorial comparison. Signal_low supplies an additional exposure condition and rescue a separately prepared comparison; its name does not establish intervention specificity or a pathway position.

Each form has six conditions, four independently prepared cultures per condition and two times per culture: 48 rows per form. Culture identifiers are unique; matching numeric suffixes across conditions does not create paired donors. A culture measured twice remains one biological unit. Cells, RNA counts, technical channels and aliquots are measurements within a culture. The same culture's signaling columns represent matched aliquots at 0, 10 and 30 minutes; they are a separate short-time experiment associated with that culture, repeated as metadata in both endpoint rows. Do not count the repeated columns twice or mistake minutes for hours.

## Data dictionary and transformations

| Columns | Meaning and scale | Interpretation and checks |
| --- | --- | --- |
| form, condition, culture, time_h | Authored form, assigned condition, independent culture ID, endpoint hours | Keep forms separate and retain each culture across time. |
| seeded_cells, viable_cells | Initial seeded and recovered viable-cell counts | Unequal cell numbers and viability deliberately affect bulk signals. Lost cells are not profiled. |
| rna_signal_eq, rna_background_eq | RNA-equivalent teaching units before and for background | Subtract background before normalization. This is a shared RNA abundance signal, not an isoform or transcription-rate assay. |
| spike_input_eq, spike_recovered_eq | External RNA recovery reference, same equivalent-unit scale | Correct RNA as `(signal-background)*spike_input/spike_recovered`; per 1000 viable cells divides by `viable_cells/1000`. The model assumes a shared recovery effect. |
| protein_signal_ug, protein_background_ug | Recovered protein and background in micrograms | Correct as `(signal-background)/protein_recovery_fraction`; express per 1000 viable cells when comparing culture amounts. This is quantity, not a mature-function measurement. |
| protein_recovery_fraction | External recovery fraction between zero and one | The stipulated reference corrects extraction loss; it cannot establish native protein localization or pathway activity. |
| marker_positive_viable | Marker-positive count among viable cells | Percent is `100*positive/viable_cells`. It is not a potency, permanence or clinical-benefit assay. |
| functional_events_viable | Count with a separate fictional function-associated event among viable cells | Percent uses the viable denominator. This proxy is deliberately distinct from the marker, and is not mature tissue function. No joint marker/event identities are supplied. |
| perk_0_au, perk_10_au, perk_30_au | Background-corrected signal at minutes 0, 10 and 30, arbitrary units | Trapezoidal area over 0–30 minutes is `5*y0+15*y10+10*y30` in AU-minutes. Normalization assumes stable detection, not a biochemical concentration. |
| matrix_modulus_pa | Assigned ideal material modulus in pascals | No viscoelasticity, traction or geometry measurements are provided. |
| accessible_ligand_relative | Relative accessible ligand presentation | It differs with the matrix-high condition, a deliberate confound. Modulus and ligand effects cannot be separated by these comparisons alone. |

Compute per-culture quantities first, then use equal-culture means for each condition. Record which fields are raw, corrected, normalized or model-derived. Summarize all four cultures, including spread descriptively, and state the absent uncertainty calibration. A pooled cell fraction answers a differently weighted question; it does not replace the requested equal-culture summary. Use the given integer counts rather than assuming a nominal percentage from a generator equation.

## Five milestone deliverables

1. **M1, proposed week 10:** a 150–250-word question and scope note, a data dictionary in your own words, the independent unit and the principal comparison. Identify the intentional cell-number and matrix-presentation limitations.
2. **M2, proposed week 11:** a reproducible analysis plan with transformations, denominators, culture/time handling, controls present and absent, and at least two competing explanations. Separate descriptive calculations from an inferential or causal model.
3. **M3, proposed week 13:** a UTF-8 CSV with one row per culture/time and derived RNA, protein, marker and function quantities, plus a condition summary. Include a signaling summary using one trajectory per culture. Supply at least one labelled figure with axes/scales, culture-level observations and a text alternative that states the comparison, pattern and limits; the table must provide the numerical information without seeing a figure. The deterministic machine packet is a separate check, not a full portfolio evaluator.
4. **M4, proposed week 14:** a 400–700-word evidence interpretation separating molecular, signaling, cell-marker and function-proxy claims. State two alternatives, the ligand/modulus confound, viable loss and missing joint states. Design one factorial follow-up with matched accessible ligand, material measurements, independent cultures, positive/negative controls and separate marker/function readouts. State what result would distinguish the alternatives and what this synthetic experiment cannot establish.
5. **M5, proposed week 14:** a short methods note sufficient to reproduce your tables and figure, including form, file, culture unit, formulas, rounding, exclusions, any tools/AI assistance and limitations. Submitted scripts are explanatory attachments for future human review and are never executed by the platform.

These lengths and weeks are draft planning bounds, not measured workload or deadlines. A reviewer must pilot the assignment, revise burden, establish accessible equivalents and set submission formats/policies before offering credit. All needed numbers and assumptions are available in text and tables; no color, visual-only image or mouse-only action is required by this packet.

## Human analytic rubric: four distinct draft levels

Each criterion has levels 3, 2, 1 and 0. The levels are descriptors for human feedback; no weights, total score or pass threshold are authorized. The reviewer records criterion, cited portfolio evidence, level, reason and proposed feedback, and distinguishes missing evidence from a biological misconception. Machine results may inform calculation checks but cannot replace these judgments.

| Criterion | 3: full observable evidence | 2: adequate with a limited gap | 1: fragmentary evidence | 0: absent or incompatible |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Question and scope | Specific question answerable by named comparisons, layers and units; explicitly bounded to this synthetic in-vitro model. | Answerable question with one vague comparison or limit. | Relevant topic but no defined comparison or partly unanswerable question. | No question, or a clinical/causal claim unsupported by the design. |
| Evidence summary | Correct culture/time accounting and equal-culture summaries across layers; raw/derived scales and descriptive spread clearly separated. | Mostly correct summaries with one minor unit/presentation gap that preserves the comparison. | Some correct values but material omissions, pooling or replicate ambiguity. | Missing evidence or technical/cell counts treated as independent cultures in a way that changes inference. |
| Controls and alternatives | Present/absent controls identified; at least two specific alternatives matched to discriminating readouts, including ligand/modulus confounding. | Controls and two alternatives named, with one incomplete discriminating measurement. | A generic control or alternative without a test that separates it. | No controls/alternatives, or rescue/name treated as proof excluding all alternatives. |
| Quantitative reasoning | Correct background/recovery/viable denominators and culture-first transformations, units, equations and rounding; uncertainty limitations stated. | One minor computation or reporting error with reproducible method and bounded consequence. | Partial method with a major missing denominator, correction or uncertainty boundary. | No reproducible analysis or incorrect transformations that determine the central result. |
| Inference and limits | Distinguishes RNA/protein quantity, signaling, marker and separate function proxy; states loss, joint-state, mechanism, generality and clinical limits. | Main distinctions retained with one unsupported phrase or omitted limitation. | A partly supported conclusion with repeated layer conflation or overreach. | Marker, nominal rescue or synthetic pattern asserted as clinical benefit or proven mechanism. |
| Follow-up design | Testable factorial comparison with matched ligand/material/cell state, independent units, controls and distinct marker/function measurements; discriminating predictions stated. | Plausible design missing one control or explicit prediction. | A relevant next measurement with no controlled comparison or independent unit. | No testable follow-up or one unable to address the stated alternative. |
| Communication and reproducibility | Clear labels/scales, culture-level figure and complete numerical text alternative; methods reproduce tables/figure and disclose tools/AI. | Understandable accessible output with one limited labelling or methods gap. | Output interpretable with effort but incomplete alternative text, units or workflow. | Evidence inaccessible, no methods, or output not reproducible. |
