# Enzymes and cellular energetics

For a simple single-substrate enzyme, v = Vmax[S]/(Km + [S]). Vmax scales with active enzyme concentration; Km is the substrate concentration at half Vmax under the model's assumptions, and is not generally a binding dissociation constant. Catalysts lower activation barriers for forward and reverse reactions without changing ΔG° or the equilibrium constant. Cells drive unfavorable steps by coupling them to favorable reactions, often through ATP or ion gradients; pathway flux also depends on substrate supply, products, and regulation.
