# Carbonyl chemistry and acyl transfer

The polarized C=O bond leaves carbon electrophilic and oxygen nucleophilic. Aldehydes and ketones usually undergo nucleophilic addition. Carboxylic-acid derivatives can undergo nucleophilic acyl substitution because a leaving group can depart after tetrahedral-intermediate formation; leaving-group ability and resonance donation govern relative reactivity. In biochemistry, acyl transfer is often driven by activation, enzyme catalysis, or coupling to nucleotide triphosphate hydrolysis rather than by an unusually strong naked nucleophile.
