# Structure, resonance, and acid-base reactivity

Organic reactivity begins with electron distribution. Resonance contributors move electrons, not nuclei; valid contributors preserve sigma-bond connectivity and total charge. Acidity depends on the stability of the conjugate base, shaped by atom identity, resonance, induction, hybridization, and solvation. Curved arrows start at electron pairs or bonds and end where electrons move. Explicit charges and complete electron accounting prevent common mechanism errors.
