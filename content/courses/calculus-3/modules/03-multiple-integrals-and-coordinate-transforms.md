# Multiple integrals and coordinate transforms

A multiple integral accumulates a density over a region. In polar coordinates, dA=r dr dθ; the factor r is the Jacobian that accounts for the changing area of a coordinate cell. Cylindrical and spherical coordinates have their own volume Jacobians. Sketch the region before writing bounds. For tissue concentration c(x), total amount is ∫Ωc(x)dV; concentration times volume is valid only when c is uniform.
