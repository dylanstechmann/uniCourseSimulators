# Line integrals, flux, and integral theorems

A line integral accumulates a field along an oriented curve; a flux integral measures a field component crossing an oriented boundary. Green's theorem relates planar circulation or flux to an area integral, Stokes' theorem relates boundary circulation to curl through a surface, and the divergence theorem relates outward flux through a closed surface to divergence in the enclosed volume. Orientation and smoothness conditions matter. These theorems can simplify calculations and provide checks on numerical field measurements.
