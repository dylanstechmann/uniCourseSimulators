# Multivariable optimization and constraints

An unconstrained interior optimum satisfies ∇f=0, then second derivatives classify local curvature. On a smooth constraint g(x)=c, an optimum may satisfy ∇f=λ∇g. Boundary points and feasible ranges still need checking. In design problems, an objective and constraint encode different engineering statements; a mathematically optimal point outside biological or manufacturing limits is not an acceptable design.
