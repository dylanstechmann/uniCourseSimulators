# Eigenvalues and linear dynamical systems

For ẋ=Ax, eigenvectors define invariant directions and eigenvalues determine growth, decay, or oscillation along modes. Negative real parts imply asymptotic decay for a continuous-time linear system; any eigenvalue with positive real part signals instability. If A is not diagonalizable, generalized eigenvectors produce polynomial factors multiplying exponentials. A local linearization near a nonlinear equilibrium is useful only near that equilibrium and under its stability assumptions.
