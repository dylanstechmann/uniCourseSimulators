# Least squares and data fitting

When Ax=b is overdetermined, least squares chooses x minimizing ||Ax−b||². If A has full column rank, the normal equations AᵀAx=Aᵀb yield the solution. Residuals reveal mismatch but should be inspected for structure; a small residual does not validate assumptions or causal meaning. Scaling, collinearity, outliers, and uncertainty can change estimates. Weighted least squares is appropriate when measurement variance differs and weights are justified.
