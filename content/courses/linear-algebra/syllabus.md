# Linear Algebra for Modeling & Robotics

**Maturity: partial. Version: 0.3.0.** This is a personal learning project developed with substantial AI assistance, not an accredited university course.

Vectors, matrices, eigenstructure, least squares and linear transformations applied to state estimation, circuit networks and robot coordinates.

## Prerequisites

Calculus I; matrix arithmetic. Structured prerequisite relationships appear in the manifest and remain subject to author review. This package establishes no university prerequisite equivalency.

## Authored outcomes

- Solve linear systems and diagnose singularity.
- Interpret bases, rank, and null spaces.
- Use eigenvalues to describe linear dynamics.
- Fit an overdetermined model by least squares.

## Assessment and study policy

Practice includes numeric, single-choice, multiple-select, CSV upload and structured-choice checks. The lab asks for sensor estimates, errors and interpretation. Written discussion criteria are guidance, not automatically graded prose. Retries are unlimited and practice points do not establish an institutional grade, university credit or mastery. Assessments list the objectives and course outcomes their items assess; prototype objective links remain provisional.

There are 14 readings, 91 public practice items and 48 retrieval cards, plus the existing self-assessment case checklist.

## Current limitations

Four compact prototype units remain. There is no graded homework sequence, midterm, final or graded integrative project. One synthetic lab is not a laboratory sequence. Workload and accessibility have not been measured or reviewed. All uncertainty calculations depend on specified matrix scales and noise assumptions, and regularization supplies a convention rather than a new measurement. The package remains partial and unreviewed.
