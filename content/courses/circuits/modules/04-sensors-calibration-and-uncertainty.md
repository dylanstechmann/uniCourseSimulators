# Sensors, calibration, and uncertainty

A sensor maps a physical quantity into an electrical signal through a transfer function. Calibration estimates that mapping against known references; validation asks whether it works in the intended context. Hysteresis, drift, cross-sensitivity, temperature, and mounting can dominate nominal resolution. Uncertainty propagation uses derivatives for small independent errors and covariance terms when measurements are correlated. Report range, bandwidth, noise floor, calibration date, and operating conditions with a measurement.
