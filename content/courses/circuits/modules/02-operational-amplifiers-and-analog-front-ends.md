# Operational amplifiers and analog front ends

An ideal op amp has very high input impedance and gain, but real devices have finite bandwidth, offset, bias current, noise, and output limits. Negative feedback sets closed-loop gain when the amplifier operates linearly. Instrumentation amplifiers provide high input impedance and common-mode rejection, but electrode mismatch, lead motion, and reference choices still matter. A correct design checks common-mode range, input-referred noise, saturation recovery, and patient isolation requirements before trusting the signal.
