# LTI systems, convolution, and transfer functions

A linear time-invariant system is characterized by its impulse response h(t); output is the convolution y=x*h. With zero initial conditions, Laplace transforms turn differential equations into algebraic transfer functions G(s)=Y(s)/U(s). Poles govern natural modes; zeros shape input-output cancellation and frequency response. Initial conditions and nonlinearities are excluded by the standard transfer-function model and must be handled separately when they matter.
