# Feedback, stability, and robustness

For a negative unity-feedback loop with forward transfer G, closed-loop response is T=G/(1+G). Stability depends on roots of the closed-loop characteristic equation, not merely on whether components are individually stable. Feedback can improve tracking and reject disturbances but can amplify measurement noise or destabilize a delayed plant. Robustness asks whether performance and stability persist under parameter uncertainty and unmodeled dynamics. Saturation and anti-windup matter in physical controllers.
