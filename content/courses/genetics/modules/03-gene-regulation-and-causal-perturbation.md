# Gene regulation and causal perturbation

A cis-regulatory sequence affects a linked allele on the same DNA molecule; trans-acting factors diffuse and can act on multiple alleles. Reporter assays isolate regulatory fragments but may remove native chromatin context. CRISPR interference can reduce transcription without cutting DNA, while knockout can disrupt coding or regulatory function. Strong causal inference combines a defined perturbation, matched controls, orthogonal readouts, and rescue where feasible.
