# Segregation, penetrance, and probability

Mendelian models assume defined alleles, segregation, and (for simple ratios) complete penetrance, no viability differences, and independent loci. A Punnett square is a probability model rather than a prediction of each family. Incomplete penetrance means some individuals with a genotype do not show the phenotype; variable expressivity changes severity. Bayes' rule updates risk when family history or a diagnostic test contributes evidence.
