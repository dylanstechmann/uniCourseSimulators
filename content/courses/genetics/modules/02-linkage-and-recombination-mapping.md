# Linkage and recombination mapping

Linked loci on the same chromosome are inherited together more often than unlinked loci. Recombination frequency is the fraction of recombinant gametes or progeny in a suitable cross. For short intervals, 1% recombination is approximately 1 centimorgan, but multiple crossovers can make distant loci appear less recombinant; the observable fraction cannot exceed 0.5. Mapping requires clear parental phase and correct identification of parental and recombinant classes.
