# Stoichiometry and solution concentration

A balanced equation conserves atoms and charge. Stoichiometric coefficients relate moles, not masses or volumes directly; use molar mass or concentration to convert. The limiting reagent sets maximum product. In dilute solutions, molarity is moles of solute per liter of solution. During a dilution with no reaction or loss, moles of solute are conserved, so C₁V₁ = C₂V₂ when units are consistent.
