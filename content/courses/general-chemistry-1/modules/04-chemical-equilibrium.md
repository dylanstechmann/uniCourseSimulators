# Chemical equilibrium

At equilibrium, forward and reverse rates are equal, not necessarily zero. For aA + bB ⇌ cC + dD, Q = [C]^c[D]^d/([A]^a[B]^b), omitting pure solids and liquids. If Q < K, net reaction proceeds toward products; if Q > K, toward reactants. K changes with temperature, while concentration or pressure perturbations change Q and the equilibrium composition. Equilibrium constants are dimensionless when written with activities; concentration forms are approximations for sufficiently dilute solutions.
