# Atoms, bonding, and molecular shape

Electron configuration organizes valence behavior; effective nuclear charge, shielding, and subshell occupancy help explain periodic trends. Lewis structures track valence electrons and formal charge. VSEPR predicts approximate molecular geometry from electron domains, while molecular orbital ideas explain bond order and magnetism more deeply. Geometry and polarity influence solubility, intermolecular forces, and molecular recognition, but shape predictions should not be mistaken for full quantum descriptions.
