# Oxygen transfer and consumption

Dissolved oxygen is supplied by gas-liquid transfer and consumed by cells. A common model is OTR=kLa(C*−C), while oxygen uptake rate depends on viable cell density and specific consumption qO₂. Steady operation requires transfer capacity to meet demand at the target dissolved concentration. Increasing agitation or gas flow can raise kLa but also alter shear, bubbles, foam, and product quality. Measure oxygen where cells experience it, not only at a bulk probe.
