# Mixing, shear, and scale-up

Scale-up cannot preserve every variable at once. Reynolds number, power per volume, tip speed, mixing time, and gas-transfer capacity each capture different aspects of a reactor. Cells may be sensitive to local shear, bubble rupture, or repeated exposure to gradients. Geometric similarity alone does not guarantee equivalent physiology. Scale-down models can recreate large-scale gradients at tractable volume and help identify failure mechanisms before full scale-up.
