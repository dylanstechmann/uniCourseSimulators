# Growth kinetics and process mass balance

In a balanced exponential phase, dX/dt=μX and X(t)=X₀e^(μt). Doubling time td=ln2/μ. Real cultures transition through lag, exponential, nutrient-limited, and decline phases; density, media exchange, oxygen, and waste alter μ. A growth curve should report viable cell concentration, sampling plan, and passage context. A fitted rate from one interval is not a universal property of the cell line.
