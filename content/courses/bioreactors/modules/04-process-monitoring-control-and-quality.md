# Process monitoring, control, and quality

A reproducible culture process defines critical process parameters (CPPs) and critical quality attributes (CQAs), with sensors and sampling that can detect excursions. pH, dissolved oxygen, temperature, agitation, feed, viable cell density, and metabolite profiles may be monitored. Sensor calibration, sterile sampling, data integrity, and alarm response are part of the process. Acceptance criteria should be established from function and variability, not selected after seeing the batch results.
