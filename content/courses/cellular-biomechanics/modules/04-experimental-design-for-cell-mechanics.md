# Experimental design for cell mechanics

Hydrogels can vary in stiffness, ligand coupling, swelling, pore size, and viscoelastic relaxation. A mechanobiology design should measure the material actually experienced by cells and include a factorial or otherwise controlled strategy for covarying inputs. Donor, passage, seeding density, and batch can create nested variability. Predefine the primary endpoint, include positive and negative controls, and image fields using blinded selection. Report effect size and uncertainty, not only representative images.
