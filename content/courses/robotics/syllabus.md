# Robotics & Mechatronics

**Maturity: partial. Version: 0.4.0.** Personal hobby and learning project with substantial AI assistance; no university equivalence or institutional credit.

Original instruction develops labeled frames and calibration, Jacobian velocity and force mapping, constrained inverse branches, point-mass dynamics and energy, motor and feedback limits, trajectories and conditional stopping bounds, and model-based sensing evidence. Preserved planar-arm and gearbox lessons remain. An original frame text map has a verbal description.

## Prerequisites

Linear Algebra and Differential Equations; Circuits recommended. Manifest relationships are provisional and establish no university prerequisite equivalency.

## Authored outcomes

- Transform coordinates between robot and task frames.
- Solve forward and inverse kinematics for a planar arm.
- Relate actuator torque, gearing, and load.
- Design a sensing and control loop with failure limits.

## Assessment policy

There are 14 readings, 91 public practice items and 48 cards, plus the preserved case. Each new lesson has six numeric and three conceptual choices. The lab includes eight grouped velocity summaries, inverse-rate and limiter calculations, structured choices and multiple-select interpretation. Seven preserved numeric items require units; new items request bare numbers in explicit scales. Significant figures, dimensions, planner/controller code, diagrams and written diagnoses are not automatically scored. Unlimited retries provide study feedback, not course grades. Outcome links remain provisional.

## Current limitations

Four compact readings remain. One data lab is not a laboratory sequence; graded homework, exams and a cumulative graded project remain absent. Workload and qualified subject/accessibility review are outstanding. The lab uses frozen Jacobians, a common speed limiter and deterministic report variants, without a dynamic robot simulation. Inward and tangent are fixed base x/y direction labels, not exact radial/tangent directions at every bent pose. No physical operation, protective-function validation or standards compliance is established. The preserved case's torque wording requires a dynamic model: finite force mapping does not universally diverge. Its redundancy option requires usable extra degrees of freedom or task relaxation. The package stays partial, unreviewed and formative-only.
