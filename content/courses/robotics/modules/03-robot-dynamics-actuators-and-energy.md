# Robot dynamics, actuators, and energy

Robot dynamics relate joint torques to acceleration, velocity, configuration, and external loads. A DC motor's torque and speed depend on current and back-emf; gearing trades speed for torque and adds inertia, backlash, and loss. Mechanical power is P=τω. Peak, continuous, and thermal ratings differ. Biomedical devices must also consider contact forces, fail-safe states, and biological variability rather than sizing only to nominal loads.
