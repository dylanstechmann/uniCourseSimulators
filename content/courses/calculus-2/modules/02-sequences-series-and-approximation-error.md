# Sequences, series, and approximation error

A series is the limit of its partial sums; terms approaching zero is necessary but not sufficient for convergence. Geometric, p-series, comparison, ratio, root, and alternating tests answer different structural questions. Taylor expansion approximates a smooth function near a center, with error controlled by omitted derivatives over a stated interval. Truncating a series should be justified by an error bound when a numerical result supports a design or scientific conclusion.
