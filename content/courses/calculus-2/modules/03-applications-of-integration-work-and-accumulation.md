# Applications of integration: work and accumulation

A definite integral accumulates a changing quantity over a domain. Work by a one-dimensional position-dependent force is W=∫ₐᵇF(x)dx; area, volume, average value, and exposure use the same accumulation idea with different measures. Bounds encode the physical domain, and units multiply through the integral. For measured data, numerical quadrature is an approximation whose error depends on sampling and curvature.
