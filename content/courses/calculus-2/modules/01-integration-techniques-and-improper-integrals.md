# Integration techniques and improper integrals

Integration by substitution reverses the chain rule; integration by parts reverses the product rule. Partial fractions decompose rational functions once the denominator is factored. Improper integrals require limits at infinite endpoints or singularities; finite-looking area is not sufficient to guarantee convergence. In pharmacokinetics, an exposure integral AUC = ∫₀∞ C(t)dt converges only if concentration declines fast enough.
