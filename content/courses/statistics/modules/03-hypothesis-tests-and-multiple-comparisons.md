# Hypothesis tests and multiple comparisons

A p-value is the probability, under the null model and assumptions, of data at least as incompatible with that model as observed. It is not the probability the null is true, nor a measure of effect size. Multiple testing increases false-positive opportunities; family-wise error or false-discovery-rate procedures address different goals. Pre-specifying primary outcomes, contrasts, and exclusion rules reduces researcher degrees of freedom. A statistically detectable effect can be too small to matter practically.
