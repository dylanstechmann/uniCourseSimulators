# First-order models and time constants

A first-order linear model ẋ = −k(x−x*) has equilibrium x* and solution x(t)=x*+(x₀−x*)e^(−kt). Its time constant τ=1/k is the time to complete about 63% of a step response. Exponential models appear in washout, relaxation, charging, and simple population dynamics. Units constrain k to inverse time. Initial conditions are part of a complete prediction; the differential equation alone gives a family of trajectories.
