# Second-order systems and mechanical response

A mass-spring-damper system follows m ẍ + c ẋ + kx = F(t). Natural frequency ωn=√(k/m) and damping ratio ζ=c/(2√(km)) characterize the unforced response. ζ<1 gives oscillatory decay, ζ=1 critical damping, and ζ>1 overdamping. Units and free-body diagrams prevent sign errors. Real tissue and robotics mechanisms may be nonlinear or viscoelastic, so a linear spring-damper is a local or frequency-limited approximation.
