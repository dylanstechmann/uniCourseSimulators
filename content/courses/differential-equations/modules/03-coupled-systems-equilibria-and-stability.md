# Coupled systems, equilibria, and stability

A coupled model ẋ=f(x) can be linearized near equilibrium x* using the Jacobian J=∂f/∂x|x*. Eigenvalues of J describe local modes: negative real parts indicate local decay, positive real parts indicate instability, and purely imaginary or zero modes require further analysis. Nullclines help visualize equilibria in two dimensions. Local stability does not guarantee global behavior; other equilibria, boundaries, and nonlinear limit cycles may matter.
