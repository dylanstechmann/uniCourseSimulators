# Momentum, collisions, and impulse

Linear momentum is p=mv; impulse J=∫Fdt equals change in momentum. Total momentum is conserved when net external impulse is negligible, even when kinetic energy is not. An elastic collision conserves kinetic energy as well; an inelastic collision converts some organized kinetic energy into deformation, heat, or sound. In impact biomechanics, force peaks depend on compliance and pulse duration, so the impulse alone does not specify tissue injury risk.
