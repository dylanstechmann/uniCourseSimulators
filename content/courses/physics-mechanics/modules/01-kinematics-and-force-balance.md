# Kinematics and force balance

Kinematics describes motion; dynamics connects motion to interactions. Newton's second law ΣF=ma applies to the net external force on a defined system. A free-body diagram includes only forces acting on that system, not the forces it exerts. Coordinate choices should simplify constraints and signs should be stated. In tissue or device mechanics, effective mass and force may depend on scale and constitutive behavior, so identify the system boundary first.
