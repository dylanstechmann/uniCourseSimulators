# Rotation, torque, and material response

Torque is τ=r×F and depends on the reference axis and lever arm. Rotational dynamics uses Στ=Iα for a rigid body about a fixed axis. For small elastic deformation, engineering stress σ=F/A and strain ε=ΔL/L; Young's modulus E=σ/ε in a linear uniaxial regime. Biological tissues are often anisotropic, nonlinear, and viscoelastic, so a single elastic modulus is only a defined test-condition summary.
