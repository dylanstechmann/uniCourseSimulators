# Stem cells, niches, and regenerative capacity

Stem-cell aging may involve intrinsic damage, altered epigenetic state, and changes in niche signals, immune environment, or systemic physiology. A larger stem-cell pool does not guarantee effective regeneration if function, engraftment, or lineage balance is impaired. Lineage tracing, clonal analysis, transplantation, and perturbation answer different questions and each introduces selection or context effects. Functional recovery should be measured at the tissue level as well as by cell markers.
