# Aging as a systems process

Aging is heterogeneous across species, tissues, and individuals. Mechanistic frameworks include genomic instability, epigenetic change, altered proteostasis, mitochondrial dysfunction, senescence, stem-cell exhaustion, and dysregulated nutrient sensing; these interact rather than forming a single linear pathway. A biomarker may predict risk without being a causal driver or a valid surrogate endpoint. Study design should define age, sex, strain, environment, tissue, and functional outcome explicitly.
